# 01 — Lab Overview

## Purpose

The Enterprise Infrastructure & Automation Lab is a controlled,
virtualized environment designed to develop and demonstrate practical
skills in infrastructure engineering, networking, system administration,
automation, monitoring, security, and troubleshooting.

## Lab Philosophy

The lab follows an engineering lifecycle:

```text
Design
  ↓
Build
  ↓
Configure
  ↓
Validate
  ↓
Monitor
  ↓
Automate
  ↓
Break
  ↓
Troubleshoot
  ↓
Recover
  ↓
Document
  ↓
Improve
